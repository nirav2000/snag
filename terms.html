Snag terms of use

Effective 27 September 2026.

Service

Snag helps users record, share and track defects, work items and completion evidence. It is a record-keeping and collaboration tool, not a substitute for professional surveying, building-control, legal or safety advice.

Accounts and access

Users are responsible for sign-in details and invitation links. Project owners control membership and should revoke access when it is no longer required.

User content

Users remain responsible for uploaded content and must have the right to share it with project participants.

Payments

Home Project is sold per property as shown at checkout. Snag Pro is a recurring plan where offered. Stripe processes payments.

Availability

The service may change as it develops. Export important handover records and do not use Snag as the sole repository for safety-critical documentation.
