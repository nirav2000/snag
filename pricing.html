Pricing is being refined

We are separating product access from the final price decision.

The current priority is to make the upgrade point feel natural: people should already have meaningful data in Snag before they are asked to pay. Final public prices can be added here once the free limit and collaboration trigger are agreed.

How the model is intended to work

Why start free?

A blank defect tracker has little value. Snag becomes useful only after someone has captured enough real issues to rely on it.

Why charge for collaboration?

Sharing, assigning and coordinating work is the point where Snag starts replacing fragmented messages and spreadsheets.

Why a separate multi-project tier?

People managing several properties, sites or teams get materially more value and need additional controls, reporting and administration.
